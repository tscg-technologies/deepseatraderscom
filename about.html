ABOUT DEEP SEA TRADERS · UPPAL, HYDERABAD

Empowering Local Seafood Businesses with Direct Wholesale Supply

Deep Sea Traders is a registered B2B wholesale seafood distributor headquartered in Uppal, Hyderabad. We bridge the gap between primary coastal fishing harbors, freshwater aqua farms, and local businesses across Greater Hyderabad.

Our core mission is straightforward: Empower local seafood shops, retail fish stalls, supermarkets, and restaurants by delivering daily fresh catches at competitive mandi rates, eliminating multiple layers of intermediaries that inflate costs.

We specialize exclusively in high-demand, high-margin categories: Fresh Sea Fish (Vanjaram, Pomfret, Tuna), Sweetwater Fish (Korameenu, Rohu, Catla), and Export-Grade Prawns. Every batch is graded, packed in calibrated crushed-ice crates, and delivered straight to retail doors between 4:00 AM and 8:00 AM.
